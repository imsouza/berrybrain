# Author Confirmation Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist contains declarations that only the authors can truthfully make. It is deliberately
unsigned. No unchecked item is represented as confirmed by an automated system.

Complete this checklist immediately before uploading the manuscript to JBCS.

## Authorship And Originality

- [ ] Both authors approve the final title, abstract, manuscript, declarations, references, figures, tables,
      supplementary files, and cover letter.
- [ ] Both authors confirm that they meet the journal's authorship requirements and that no qualifying author
      has been omitted.
- [ ] Both authors confirm that Mateus Almeida de Souza is first and corresponding author and
      Leonardo Jose Silvestre is second author and academic supervisor.
- [ ] Leonardo Jose Silvestre supplied his ORCID, and its checksum and public identity were verified.
- [ ] Both authors confirm that the manuscript has not been published and is not under review by another
      journal or conference.
- [ ] Both authors have disclosed any earlier paper, report, preprint, thesis, or public version that overlaps
      with this manuscript.

## Evidence And Reproducibility

- [ ] I inspected the retained command outputs and confirm that every reported number is traceable
      to the cited evidence artifact.
- [ ] I confirm that synthetic fixtures and exploratory findings are labeled as such and are not
      described as real-user or confirmatory evidence.
- [ ] I confirm that no private note, credential, API key, personal identifier, or prohibited data
      is present in the submission package or public evidence archive.
- [ ] I will freeze the exact commit, lockfiles, prompts, non-secret configuration, evidence files,
      hashes, and reproduction instructions before creating the DOI-bearing archive.
- [ ] I will replace the pending Zenodo statement only after the DOI deposit actually exists and
      has been independently checked against the GitHub research artifact.

## Declarations

- [ ] Each author confirms that the CRediT roles in `main.tex` accurately describe that author's contribution.
- [ ] Both authors confirm that the statement of no specific external funding is complete, including any
      scholarship, grant, equipment support, or project funding that requires acknowledgement.
- [ ] Both authors confirm that the competing-interest declaration is complete.
- [ ] I confirm that all copyrighted material, software licenses, datasets, and benchmark terms
      permit the uses and distribution described in the manuscript.
- [ ] I confirm that the ethics statement matches the data actually used in the reported study.
- [ ] I confirm that the generative-AI disclosure accurately covers software engineering, test
      design, research organization, writing support, verification, and human responsibility.

## Language And Presentation

- [ ] I completed a final independent English-language read-through after the last technical edit.
- [ ] I inspected the final PDF page by page for blank columns, misplaced floats, clipped content,
      unreadable figures, broken links, and incorrect author metadata.
- [ ] I confirmed that every citation resolves to the intended source and that the reference data
      remain current at submission time.

- [ ] I reviewed the current JBCS submission instructions again on the submission date.
