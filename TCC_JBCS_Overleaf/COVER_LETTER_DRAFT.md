## Letter

Dear Editors of the Journal of the Brazilian Computer Society,

Please consider the manuscript “BerryBrain: Architecture and Experimental Evaluation of an
Adaptive Knowledge Management System Based on Semantic Graphs, GraphRAG, and Human Feedback” for
the regular Articles section in the area of Information Retrieval.

The manuscript presents a self-hosted personal knowledge management architecture that transforms
Markdown notes into a governed semantic graph, combines lexical, dense, and structural retrieval,
and uses provenance, signal-derived confidence, configurable language-model judges, and reversible
human feedback. Its primary contribution is an auditable research artifact and evaluation
protocol. The paper does not claim universal superiority: controlled-fixture findings,
public-corpus baseline results, measured engineering performance, and remaining external-validity
gaps are reported separately.

The work is relevant to JBCS readers studying information retrieval, GraphRAG, knowledge graphs,
human-in-the-loop systems, and empirical software evaluation. The artifact is evaluated through
automated software tests, retrieval ablations, a public benchmark, performance measurements,
fault and security checks, accessibility checks, and a maturity assessment. The manuscript also
defines a confirmatory protocol for human annotation, judge calibration, usability, and
longitudinal adaptation; it does not populate those future outcomes before execution.

Generative AI tools assisted software engineering, code review and debugging, the design and
addition of automated tests, research organization, manuscript structure, and language revision.
The first author executed the tests, inspected retained outputs, and verified the evidence and
citations. Both authors accept responsibility for the manuscript. No AI system is listed as an
author.

Leonardo Jose Silvestre, the academic supervisor, is the second author. The source package includes
the required contribution, competing-interest, funding, acknowledgement, data-and-materials,
ethics, and AI-use statements. The exact GitHub research artifact is versioned at
<https://github.com/imsouza/berrybrain/releases/tag/jbcs-artifact-2026-08-25>. Its Zenodo deposit
remains pending, and no DOI is claimed at this stage.

The second author holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering in a Computer Science-related research
area. His details will be entered as the committed author-reviewer only after he explicitly agrees
to review up to two papers during the evaluation. Independent reviewer suggestions will be entered
only after conflict checks.
