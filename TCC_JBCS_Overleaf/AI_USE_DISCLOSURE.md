# Generative AI Use And Verification Record

## Disclosed Uses

Generative AI tools assisted these activities:

- software engineering, code review, and debugging;
- design, addition, and refinement of automated tests;
- research organization and literature-discovery support;
- manuscript structure and language revision;
- consistency checks across the source package and documentation.

## Human-Controlled Activities

The authors remain responsible for research design, execution, interpretation, and submission.
Reported metrics must come from executed commands and retained evidence. References must resolve to
real sources. AI-generated wording does not constitute validation, peer review, authorship, or an
experimental result.

The first author executed or commissioned execution of the software test suites and inspected their
outputs. Both authors must review the resulting claims and retain responsibility for errors.
Synthetic fixtures remain explicitly labeled; they are not represented as observations from real
users.

## Prohibited Representations

The package does not claim that an AI system:

- is an author or bears responsibility for the work;
- independently verified the study;
- performed human peer review or professional language review;
- supplied consent, conflict, originality, or funding declarations;
- created a DOI deposit or journal submission that does not exist.

The manuscript's `Ethics, privacy, and AI use` subsection contains the publication-facing
disclosure. This record provides the reproducibility-level detail behind that statement.
