## Submission Requirements

### Requirements Already Satisfied By The Package

- The manuscript file is PDF.
- The official JBCS template is used.
- XeLaTeX produces the validated PDF.
- Authors remain visible for single-blind review.
- The manuscript follows the journal's English style and bibliography structure.
- Available reference DOIs are included; unavailable DOI cases are documented.
- The authors can provide LaTeX source files if the paper is accepted.
- The declarations section contains contributions, interests, acknowledgements, funding, and
  availability of data and materials.
- The first author's ORCID is included on the first page.
- The second author's ORCID remains a mandatory pre-submission item.

### Requirements That Only The Authors Can Acknowledge

Both authors must personally verify before selecting the corresponding checkboxes:

- the manuscript has not been previously published;
- the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere;
- all journal instructions have been read;
- the manuscript and metadata are approved;
- funding and competing-interest declarations are complete;
- the copyright statement is accepted; and
- collection and storage of account data under the privacy statement is accepted.

These statements are not inferred from repository contents. Complete
`AUTHOR_CONFIRMATION_CHECKLIST.md` before acknowledging them in OJS.

## Comments For The Editor

Use the following factual content after both authors complete the confirmation checklist and
Leonardo accepts or declines the reviewer commitment:

> This manuscript is submitted to the regular Articles section. JBCS area: Information Retrieval.
> It presents the architecture and exploratory experimental evaluation of BerryBrain, a self-hosted
> adaptive personal knowledge management system based on semantic graphs, hybrid GraphRAG,
> provenance, configurable language-model Judges, and reversible human feedback. The manuscript
> explicitly distinguishes controlled regression evidence from external validation and does not
> claim universal superiority. Generative AI assistance in software engineering, automated-test
> design, research organization, structure, and language revision is disclosed in the manuscript.
> Both authors retain responsibility for the work.

Append the following only after it becomes fact:

- Leonardo Jose Silvestre's verified committed-reviewer details, only if he accepts;
- verified reviewer suggestions after conflict checks;
- an explanation of any overlapping preprint, thesis, report, or earlier publication;
- a special-issue name, only if the submission is actually redirected to one; and
- any policy issue that the editor must know.

## Copyright And Privacy

The CC BY 4.0 acknowledgement authorizes publication under the stated license if the article is
accepted. The privacy acknowledgement authorizes the portal to collect and store submission-account
data under its privacy statement. These are personal legal acknowledgements; an automated system
must not select them on the author's behalf.

## Upload Boundary

For initial submission, upload the validated `main.pdf` and provide the required editor comments or
cover letter. Do not upload internal planning, unsigned checklists, reviewer research, tests, or
audits as the manuscript.

If accepted, provide the minimal compilable LaTeX source package requested by the editorial team.
The full Overleaf/reproducibility ZIP is broader than that final editorial source package.
