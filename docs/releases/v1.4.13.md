# BerryBrain 1.4.13 — bounded recovery and clearer workspace

Product maintenance only; no new TCC/research experiments.

## Changes

- Deterministic recovery scans at most 50 exhausted jobs/minute and admits one
  retry/5 minutes while the foreground queue has room. At most three additional
  cycles (5/30/120-minute cooldowns), preserving job IDs and attempt history.
- Source hashes, semantic fingerprints and Judge timestamp/configuration
  versions guard replay. Completed maintenance replaces historical failures
  only when scopes match. Superseded is not reported as completed.
- Permanent provider errors stop retries. Graph-statistics timeout guidance
  correctly identifies local API/database computation, not cloud inference.
- Alerts count all unread notifications. Opening the panel does not clear them;
  read actions preserve history and update the badge only on success.
- Manually created insights get a first-visible-visit highlight in the graph,
  scoped to the current workspace/browser. Existing shapes/colors remain intact.
- Compact Home operations switch between processing, connections and activity.
  Lightweight accessible charts show actual assimilation, inventory and job
  states, without invented trends or extra data requests.

## Operational limits

Recovery is bounded, not a promise to repair every historical failure. Removed
models, invalid keys, quota exhaustion, changed configuration and unknown errors
may require operator action. No bulk retry or model/provider substitution occurs.
Retries can incur the configured provider's ordinary costs. Disable the supervisor
with `automatic_job_recovery_enabled=false` through the settings API.

No schema migration, history deletion or note creation by recovery. Back up
before deployment and retain previous images for rollback. Review
[API contracts](../api.md) and the
[validation checklist](../reviews/2026-10-12-autonomous-recovery-dashboard.md)
for test evidence and deployment status.
